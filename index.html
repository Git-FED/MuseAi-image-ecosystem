    README

    WHAT IT IS

    The Muse AI Image Ecosystem is a working image pipeline drawn the way the ecosystem is supposed to look: a top-down satellite view on a dark grid, rooms as blocks, corridors like streets, and AI agents as little moving dots travelling room to room. You write a brief; the ecosystem turns it into a finished image, stage by stage, in the open — and shows you the exact prompt it built along the way.

    THE SIX ROOMS

    • R01 Prompt Office — expands your one-line brief into a full descriptive prompt.
    • R02 Style Lab — locks the style, mood, lighting and palette.
    • R03 Composition Bay — frames the shot for the format you picked: square, landscape or portrait.
    • R04 Render Engine — generates the image; on this Pages build it renders live on a canvas in your browser, driven by your brief.
    • R05 Detail Forge — sharpens detail language and applies your keep-out list.
    • R06 Gallery Vault — where finished work lands.

    HOW THE AGENTS BEHAVE

    At rest the grid is quiet: 4 agents, drifting slowly along the streets. The moment a pipeline run fires, traffic ramps with every stage — more dots join the grid and every dot speeds up, up to all 30 agents at full pace while the Render Engine works. When the run ends, the grid settles back down to idle. The agent count in the status strip is live.

    RUN IT

    Open the Pages site, write a brief, pick a style and mood (or type your own), choose a format and detail level, and hit RUN PIPELINE. The rooms light up in order, the Render Engine draws your image, and it appears in the vault panel — download the image, or copy the prompt the rooms built for it.
