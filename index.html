      README

      WHAT IT IS

      The Muse AI Image Ecosystem is a working image pipeline drawn the way the ecosystem is supposed to look: a top-down satellite view on a dark grid, rooms as blocks, corridors like streets, and AI agents as little moving dots travelling room to room. You write a brief; the ecosystem turns it into a finished image, stage by stage, in the open — and shows you the exact prompt it built along the way.

      THE SIX ROOMS

      • R01 Prompt Office — expands your one-line brief into a full descriptive prompt.
      • R02 Style Lab — locks the style, mood, lighting and palette.
      • R03 Composition Bay — frames the shot for the format you picked: square, landscape or portrait.
      • R04 Render Engine — generates the image; on this Pages build it renders live on a canvas in your browser. The painter reads the subject in your brief — a cat, a rocket, a castle, a coastline each get their own scene — and trees appear only when the brief actually asks for a forest.
      • R05 Detail Forge — sharpens detail language and applies your keep-out list.
      • R06 Gallery Vault — where finished work lands, and where Photo Scout, the Vault's search worker, files the real openly licensed photos it finds for your brief.

      WHO THE AGENTS ARE — AND WHERE THEY DEPLOY FROM

      Job work is done by 12 named agents, two per room, and every one of them deploys from the same place: the Deployment Bay at the centre of the map. Quill + Scribe work R01 Prompt Office, Palette + Mood R02 Style Lab, Frame + Rule R03 Composition Bay, Forge + Kiln R04 Render Engine, Hone + Polish R05 Detail Forge, and Vault + Courier R06 Gallery Vault — Courier also runs the carry from R04 to R06 through the bay. When a stage fires, that room's pair leaves the bay, travels the streets to the room, and works at the room's workstation; when the job closes, everyone returns to the bay. Each agent's name rides with it on the map, its room shows what it is doing, and its card, the Agent Inspector and the feed all show the same live task string, so there is one answer to “who is doing what” everywhere you look.

      THE LIVE ENGINE (BACKEND FOR THIS STATIC PAGE)

      GitHub Pages has no server to add, so the backend lives in the page as a dispatcher and event store: it creates a numbered job from your brief, deploys each stage's crew out of the Deployment Bay before the stage advances, heartbeats once a second while a run is live, appends every deployment, arrival, handoff and filing to a timestamped event log (the Deployment Feed and terminal), and sends agents back to the bay as they finish. The 18 smaller dots are street traffic only — they ramp from 4 slow at rest toward full pace during a run, but they never carry a job. The full server version of this ecosystem is the Space; this Page is its live public face.

      RUN IT

      Open the Pages site, write a brief, pick a style and mood (or type your own), choose a format and detail level, and hit RUN PIPELINE. The rooms light up in order, the Render Engine draws your image, Photo Scout files real photos matching your brief under it, and it all appears in the vault panel — download the image, or copy the prompt the rooms built for it.
